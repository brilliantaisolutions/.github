# Brilliant AI Solutions

**Artificial Intelligence | Real Solutions**

AI strategy, governance, and research infrastructure advisory for higher education. Based in New York City, serving colleges and universities nationwide.

---

### The challenge

Most universities face an **AI gap**: rapidly growing demand for GPU capacity and generative AI tools, set against the realities of security, compliance, cost, and power. We help institutions move from reactive experimentation to intentional, well-governed leadership.

### What we do

| Service | What it delivers |
|---|---|
| **AI Strategy & Readiness** | A clear, multi-year AI roadmap aligned with academic and research priorities |
| **GenAI Governance** | Policies, frameworks, and governance bodies for responsible, secure AI use |
| **HPC & Research Computing** | Assessment of GPU capacity, power, and service models across campus, cloud, and shared resources |
| **Peer Benchmarking** | Competitive analysis against peer institutions to surface investment gaps and opportunities |
| **Secure Research Data** | Advisory on secure data enclaves and regulated-data environments |

### How we work

Our **Discovery Engagement** gives leadership a defensible plan in weeks, not months:

1. **Discovery** — review usage data and interview faculty, researchers, and IT leaders
2. **Benchmarking** — measure standing against leading peer institutions
3. **Gap analysis** — identify dependencies, risks, and funding opportunities
4. **Roadmap** — deliver a 3–5 year implementation plan and service model, ready for leadership and trustees
